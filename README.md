### About me

Financial analyst specializing in FP&A: forecasting, budgeting, and variance analysis. Active in equities and options trading, with a strong interest in crypto, asset tokenization, and AI-driven financial tools.

### What I focus on

| Area | What that looks like |
| --- | --- |
| 📊 **FP&A** | Budgeting, rolling forecasts, budget-vs-actual variance analysis, driver-based models |
| 📈 **Equities & options** | Fundamental and technical research, options pricing and Greeks, volatility, strategy backtesting |
